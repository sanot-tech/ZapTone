# 📜 Code of Conduct

## 🤝 Our promise

We want ZapTone to be a place where people feel welcome. No matter who you
are or what you know, you are treated with respect.

## ✅ What we expect

- 💬 Be kind and patient. Everyone was new once.
- 🧠 Be honest about mistakes, and gentle about pointing them out.
- 🎯 Argue about the code, never about the person.
- 🌍 Remember that a simple English sentence is easier for everyone.

## 🚫 What is not allowed

- 👊 Harassment of any kind
- 🗣️ Sexual language or images
- 🧟 Hate speech or personal attacks
- 📢 Spam, and advertising other tools unasked
- 🔓 Posting someone's private information

## ⚖️ What happens when someone breaks these rules

1. A maintainer writes a friendly note and asks for a change.
2. If it happens again, that person gets a temporary ban from the repo.
3. If it keeps happening, the ban becomes permanent.

We will always explain what happened and what to change.

## 📬 Reporting

Write to the maintainer privately, or use GitHub's report button on a
comment. Every report is read, and we will not share your name without
your permission.
